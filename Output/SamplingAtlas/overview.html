Genomic sampling maps · Public genomic inventory · Genomic summary for these species

80 species with at least 50 dated specimens through 1930 in the western Atlantic, Caribbean and Gulf of Mexico. Eligibility sums specimens across stages. Undated specimens do not confer eligibility.

Every map uses the same extent: 100°W to 50°W longitude, 5°N to 60°N latitude. Grid: 2° × 2°. No zooming or cropping between species, stages or years.

One map per species combines all eligible years and all life stages, including undated lots. Numbered cells connect to their collection-year labels; parentheses show specimen counts for that cell and year. Colors show total specimens per cell, not abundance. Enlarge labels for a scrollable view; the geographic extent stays fixed.

Hatched cells include approximate locations. Missing coordinates are estimated from station, locality, island or broader location labels. These display positions can be much less precise than a grid cell; they are not verified collecting sites. Frame counts separate recorded coordinates from estimates. Original coordinates remain unchanged, and estimates are excluded from precise-location candidate screens.

Adults / non-larval, larvae and mixed lots are pooled on these maps. Undated samples are included and labeled Undated in their cells. Use the year-by-year atlas to inspect stages separately.
