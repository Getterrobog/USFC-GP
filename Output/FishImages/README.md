# Image URLs for the 80 candidate fish species

Open [the workbook](fish_images_80_species.xlsx), [CSV](fish_images_80_species.csv),
or [JSON for webpage integration](fish_images_80_species.json).

There are 78 verified FishBase image URLs: 54 selected images have individual
CC BY-NC 4.0 licenses, and 24 require permission. Two separately flagged external
illustrations provide leads for *Prionotus martis* and *Myctophum opalinum*.
The latter's taxonomy remains unresolved. Images include photographs and drawings.

Use `eligible_for_noncommercial_embedding` to select verified, credited FishBase
images with documented reuse terms. Display `attribution` with links to
`image_page_url` and `license_url`. External leads remain review items.

[Methods, source details and reproduction commands](../../docs/fish_image_catalog.md)
explain the selection rules, licensing checks and limitations. No images are
bundled or inserted into existing atlas pages by this step.
