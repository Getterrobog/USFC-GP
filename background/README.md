# Scientific background

Read [one_page_proposal.txt](one_page_proposal.txt) for the motivation and three
aims of the historical genomics study. It proposes comparisons across approximately
12–20 species and 4–6 regional assemblages to investigate evolutionary responses
to coastal change.

This is the scientific proposal, not a report of completed genomic analyses.
The [species selection plan](../docs/species_selection_plan.md) translates it into
evidence requirements and decisions. The [atlas](../Output/SamplingAtlas/README.md)
and [historical screen](../Output/SpeciesSelection/README.md) provide the current
museum-sampling evidence for those decisions.
